Social Work Practices

Seven frontline practice cases developed through Qifu Social Work Service Centre's community-based drug social work.

01Seven Practices
01Binhai Subdistrict

A cultural and creative approach to immersive drug education

Binhai brings together coastal tourism, cultural creativity, and digital tools to turn everyday city walks into accessible drug-education journeys. Its Drug Prevention Cultural and Creative Street connects study visits, local businesses, and a professional volunteer network.

  • Drug prevention + cultural tourism
  • Immersive study and public education
  • Business and volunteer partnerships
Practice resultNearly 80 visiting delegations and close to 10,000 participants
02Xiagang Subdistrict

The 3-3-3 model for recovery and social reintegration

The Recovery and Reintegration Workshop combines early prevention with sustained support after treatment. Its 3-3-3 model responds to survival, livelihood, and development needs through individual, family, and environmental support, alongside physical, psychological, and social-adaptation review.

  • Individual, family, and environmental support
  • Twenty-seven structured service steps
  • Continuous recovery and reintegration planning
Practice resultRecognised as a Fujian demonstration site for community rehabilitation
03Lianqian Subdistrict

Youth-centred prevention across schools and communities

A Party-led, multi-actor governance model combines school partnerships, creative competitions, short films, public legal education, parent–child media, street experiments, and online campaigns to create a layered prevention system for young people.

  • Drug-Free Campus Alliance
  • Youth arts and creative-media programmes
  • Integrated online and offline communication
Practice result163 activities, 48,459 engagements, and more than 180 media reports in three years
04Lujiang Subdistrict

Community co-governance in a historic urban district

Lujiang works with businesses, retired police officers, delivery riders, and community volunteers to respond to the distinctive needs of a busy historic district. Shared patrols and public engagement embed prevention in neighbourhood life.

  • Community drug-prevention patrols
  • Retired police volunteer team
  • Delivery-rider prevention network
Practice result932 volunteer hours reaching 4,332 people
05Community Cultural Outreach

Drug Prevention +: connecting tourism, heritage, and public life

The Drug Prevention + approach takes prevention into places where people already gather. Cycling city walks, taxis, marathons, folk performances, lantern art, and opera make public education visible, participatory, and culturally grounded.

  • Drug prevention + tourism
  • Drug prevention + intangible heritage
  • Participatory public communication
Practice resultPrevention becomes part of everyday cultural and civic experience
06Xiamen University Partnership

Young partners and volunteer educators for drug prevention

In partnership with Xiamen University Law School, Qifu trains young volunteer educators in drug knowledge, refusal skills, programme design, and public communication. Teams then deliver activities across schools, communities, workplaces, families, venues, and online spaces.

  • Youth Drug Prevention Partnership
  • Volunteer lecturer training
  • University–community collaboration
Practice resultMore than 120 educators, 22 teams, 32 activities, and 20,000 people reached
07Xiamen & Siming District Drug Prevention Education Base

A public education hub for learning, training, and exchange

Opened in June 2022, the public education base combines permanent exhibitions with study visits, school outreach, professional training, sports and arts activities, industry-specific education, festival programmes, and weekly film screenings.

  • School and youth education
  • Training for priority industries
  • Public events and professional exchange
Practice resultA free, year-round platform for citywide drug-prevention education
