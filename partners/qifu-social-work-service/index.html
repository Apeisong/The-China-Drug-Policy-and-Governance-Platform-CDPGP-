Qifu Social Work Service Centre

Drug prevention · Rehabilitation · Community engagement

01About Qifu

Founded in 2014, Qifu Social Work Service Centre is a 5A-rated social organisation providing professional services across Fujian Province, including Fuzhou, Xiamen, Zhangzhou, Putian, and Sanming. Since 2019, Qifu has delivered community-based drug social work projects across several subdistricts in Xiamen, as well as the Xiamen and Siming District Drug Prevention Education Base. This work has built substantial locally grounded expertise in drug prevention, rehabilitation, family support, public education, and community engagement.

Working together for a caring, equal, and just society where everyone can live with dignity.

03Three-Tier Intervention Model
01

Universal prevention

Education, awareness raising, community engagement, and supportive environments for the general public.

02

Targeted prevention

Tailored support that strengthens risk awareness, informed decision-making, and harm prevention.

03

Rehabilitation and reintegration

Intensive social work support for people in rehabilitation and their families, with an emphasis on sustained recovery and social reintegration.

04 / The 3-3-3 Reintegration Model

Three dimensions, nine areas, twenty-seven practical steps.

Developed through frontline practice, the model gives social workers a systematic framework for needs assessment, support coordination, progress review, and responsive intervention throughout rehabilitation and social reintegration.

Three areas of need

Survival, basic livelihood, and personal development

Three sources of support

The individual, family, and wider social environment

Three dimensions of recovery

Mindset, physical well-being, and social adaptation

06 / Youth Engagement & Volunteering

Making young people and communities active partners in grassroots prevention.

In partnership with Xiamen University Law School, Qifu established youth-focused initiatives including the Youth Drug Prevention Partnership and a volunteer lecturer programme. Over five years, more than 120 lecturers were trained, forming 22 service teams. They delivered 32 activities across communities, schools, workplaces, families, public venues, and online spaces, reaching more than 20,000 people.

07Practice & Impact
Binhai Subdistrict

Drug prevention through culture and tourism

Experiential learning, City Walks, and creative public spaces. The Drug Prevention Creative Street has received nearly 80 visiting and research delegations.

Xiagang Subdistrict

The 3-3-3 reintegration approach

Preventive education and post-rehabilitation support form a systematic pathway for recovery and reintegration. The work was recognised as a Fujian Provincial Demonstration Site.

Lianqian Subdistrict

Multi-stakeholder community governance

163 prevention activities over three years reached 48,459 participants and received more than 180 media reports.

Lujiang Subdistrict

Community partnership and volunteering

Businesses, retired police officers, delivery riders, and residents contributed 932 volunteer hours and reached 4,332 people.

Education Base

Xiamen and Siming District Drug Prevention Education Base

A free public facility opened in June 2022, integrating public education, experiential learning, professional training, cultural communication, and community engagement.
