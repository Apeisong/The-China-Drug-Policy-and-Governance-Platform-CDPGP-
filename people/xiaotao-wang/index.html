Xiaotao Wang

01Profile

Xiaotao Wang is an Associate Professor in the School of Sociology and Head of the Department of Sociology and Social Security at Nanjing Normal University. He holds BA, MA, and PhD degrees in Sociology from Nanjing University and was a visiting doctoral researcher at the Population Research Center, the University of Texas at Austin. His research examines adolescent problem behaviours, adolescent families, fertility policy, and family adjustment, with recent work on digital addiction, vaping, school bullying, and adolescent mental health.
