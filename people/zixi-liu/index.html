Professor Zixi Liu

Sociology of law · Cultural sociology · Drug governance · Police sociology

01Biography

Zixi Liu is Co-Director of CDPGP and Professor and Vice Dean at the School of Sociology and Anthropology, Xiamen University. Her principal fields are the sociology of law and cultural sociology, with research on digital affective economies, labour-market intermediaries, drug governance and judicial social work, and the sociology of policing.
