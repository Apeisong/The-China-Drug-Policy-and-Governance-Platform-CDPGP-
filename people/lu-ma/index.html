01Profile

Lu Ma is a PhD candidate in the Department of Sociology, School of Sociology and Anthropology at Xiamen University. Her research focuses on the relationship between penal and welfare institutions in drug governance, the development of drug control-related social organisations, and the governance of drugs within policing agencies.
