01About the platform

The China Drug Policy and Governance Platform (CDPGP) is an independent research platform dedicated to advancing evidence-based, critical, and interdisciplinary research on drug policy and governance. The Platform also promotes research on the social inclusion, social integration, and rehabilitation of people who use drugs, alongside broader questions of addiction, recovery, and the ways in which these experiences are shaped by systems of governance.

Our guiding principle

“Policy becomes stronger when evidence is shared, perspectives meet, and knowledge can move into practice.”

“Putting people and communities at the centre, understanding their lived realities, and building meaningful approaches to care, recovery, and intervention.”

02Goals of CDPGP
01 / Our goals
  • Produce high-quality research that advances understanding of drug policy, drug governance, drug use, treatment, recovery, rehabilitation, and related systems of social and criminal justice governance.
  • Strengthen the evidence base for drug policy by bringing together empirical research, policy analysis, comparative perspectives, and lived and professional experience.
  • Examine not only what drug policies are, but also how they are developed, implemented, negotiated, and experienced in practice.
  • Translate academic research into accessible knowledge that can inform policymakers, practitioners, social organisations, researchers, and the wider public.
  • Develop critical and constructive approaches to the social consequences of drug policy, including stigma, inequality, social inclusion, welfare, punishment, care, and rehabilitation.
  • Create dialogue between Chinese drug policy research and international scholarship while recognising China’s distinctive historical, institutional, cultural, and political contexts.
  • Support emerging scholars and encourage greater interdisciplinary engagement with drug policy and governance research.
02 / Building research capacity and collaboration

Capacity-building is central to the work of CDPGP. We seek to:

  • Support doctoral, postdoctoral, and early-career researchers working on drug-related issues.
  • Encourage scholars across criminology, sociology, public health, law, social work, political science, public policy, and related fields to engage with drug policy research.
  • Build collaborative networks among researchers working across China and internationally.
  • Develop partnerships with universities, research centres, practitioners, social organisations, and relevant policy communities.
  • Create opportunities for interdisciplinary workshops, seminars, visiting scholars, collaborative projects, and international exchange.
  • Make research and policy resources more accessible through publications, the Policy Library, events, databases, and public-facing research communication.
03 / Our interdisciplinary team

CDPGP brings together researchers working across Criminology, Sociology, Public Health, Social Work, Law, Political Science, Public Policy, Medicine, and related interdisciplinary fields.

This collaborative space enables researchers to exchange ideas, develop joint projects, engage with policymakers and practitioners, and better understand the systems, institutions, relationships, and social conditions through which drug policy is produced and experienced.
